Estimating & Preconstruction

Build a documented estimate from the current drawings, specifications and vendor pricing.

Quantity & material takeoffs
Measure quantities by trade, area and assembly. Organize counts, areas and lengths with plan references and measurement assumptions.
Cost estimating & pricing
Develop labor, material, equipment and subcontract cost breakdowns. Document allowances, exclusions and pricing assumptions.
Bid coordination
Maintain bid calendars, organize addenda, prepare bid packages and track vendor responses.
Scope & specification review
Compare drawings and specifications, flag gaps or overlaps, and prepare clarification lists before pricing.

Drafting, BIM & Technical Documentation

Prepare and update technical documents using approved project inputs.

CAD & BIM drafting
Draft or update floor plans, elevations, sections and model views from supplied drawings and markups.
Shop drawings & as-built updates
Draft shop drawing packages from approved design information and update record drawings from field redlines.
Permit & plan-set assembly
Organize sheets, indexes, revision logs and supporting documents for review by the responsible design professional.
Finish & material schedules
Compile finish, fixture and material schedules using approved selections and specification references.

Project Management & Controls

Keep schedule, cost, procurement and document records aligned with project decisions.

Scheduling & progress tracking
Build and update milestone schedules and look-ahead plans. Track actual progress, upcoming constraints and procurement dependencies.
RFIs & submittals
Maintain registers, route packages, track due dates and approvals, and identify items that need follow-up.
Procurement support
Request and compare vendor quotes, prepare purchase order drafts and track lead times, releases and deliveries.
Budget, forecast & change control
Track commitments, actual costs and forecasts to complete. Maintain change logs, assemble backup and report cost variances.

Construction Finance & Administration

Organize billing records and back-office workflows for client review and approval.

AP, AR & progress billing
Organize supplier invoices, subcontractor pay applications, owner billing records, retainage and lien waiver documentation.
Job cost reconciliation
Match invoices and supporting records to cost codes and flag discrepancies for the client’s accounting team.
Subcontractor onboarding & records
Maintain registers for contracts, insurance certificates, licenses and onboarding documents. Track renewals and outstanding items.
Dispatch & service coordination
Coordinate schedules for service calls, inspections and deliveries, and track confirmations and follow-up items.

Proposals, Sales & Client Support

Keep inquiries, proposals and post-project requests organized from first contact through follow-up.

Lead qualification & quote follow-up
Capture project basics, organize qualified inquiries and track outstanding quotes and next actions.
Client updates & warranty coordination
Prepare status updates, log warranty requests and route follow-up items to the responsible team.
Proposals & portfolio materials
Prepare capability statements, proposal packages, RFP responses and case studies using approved company and project information.

How we work

  1. Share the scope. Tell us what you need, the project stage and the target date.
  2. Agree on the deliverables. We define inputs, file formats, turnaround and review responsibilities with your team.
  3. Review and hand off. We document assumptions, address agreed revisions and deliver files for your team’s review and approval.
